About Me

I am a Ph.D. student in mathematics at Seoul National University.
My research interests lie in number theory, particularly quadratic forms, Siegel modular forms, and the theta correspondence. I am especially interested in the interplay between the arithmetic of quadratic lattices and the theory of automorphic forms. I also have an interest in arithmetic dynamics.

[1] Upper Bounds on the Sizes of Finite Orbits for Unramified Morphisms
Preprint, 2025.
[arXiv:2508.12639]
Abstract

We prove that for a dynamical system on an algebraic variety over $\overline{\mathbb{Q}}$ generated by finitely many formally unramified endomorphisms, it is decidable whether the forward orbit of a given point is finite. This is achieved by establishing an effective upper bound for the size of finite orbits in integral algebraic dynamical systems with formally unramified morphisms.
